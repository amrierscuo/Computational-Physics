About this layer

S2 geometry with an accepted POI layer.

The boundaries are calculated locally from the S2 quadratic cube projection. This review build can also load an exported Accepted contribution dataset without requesting private Wayfarer services.

Wayspot nominations use the primary and support photos. Accepted photo contributions use only Your Image Submission. Images flagged during privacy review are excluded from the published dataset.

L17 is drawn from zoom 16 and L14 from zoom 12 to keep mobile interaction fast.
