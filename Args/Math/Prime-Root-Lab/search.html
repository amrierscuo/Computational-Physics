NOTEBOOK 4.2 | PYTHON/PYCBC

Matched filtering in action

strain = highpass(strain, 15.0)
strain = resample_to_delta_t(strain, 1.0/2048)
psd = conditioned.psd(4)
psd = interpolate(psd, conditioned.delta_f)
snr = matched_filter(template, conditioned,
    psd=psd, low_frequency_cutoff=20)

The page implements the numerical analogue in JavaScript with a local FFT, Welch PSD and noise weighting.

NOTEBOOK 4.3 | CONSISTENCY

χ² and ranking

chisq = power_chisq(hp, data, nbins, psd,
    low_frequency_cutoff=20.0)
dof = nbins * 2 - 2
chisq /= dof
ranking = newsnr(abs(snr), chisq)

In the lab, bands are built from the whitened template power and the ranking uses the same newSNR penalty form.
