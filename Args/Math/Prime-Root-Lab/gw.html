ODW ANALYSIS CHAIN

From photodiode to time-frequency map

  1. Optical responsePhotodiode and control signals are calibrated into strain.
  2. Data qualityNonstationary noise and glitches require checks and vetoes.
  3. PSD and whiteningWelch estimates the floor; whitening compensates the expected spectral scale.
  4. Band-passThe useful band excludes uninformative frequencies. This view remains at 0-500 Hz.
  5. STFT or Q-transformThe STFT has fixed resolution. The Q-transform uses variable windows and is often clearer for chirps and transients.

1 | PRIME MAPPING

From percentage to simulated strain

\[q_j(t)=\frac{N_j(t)}{N}-0.25\]

\[\delta_p(t)=[q_A(t)+q_C(t)]-[q_B(t)+q_D(t)]\]

\[h_{\mathrm{sim}}(t)=10^{-21}\frac{\delta_p(t)}{\max |\delta_p|}\]

Both quantities are dimensionless. The 10^-21 factor is a chosen teaching calibration, not a dataset result.

2 | 2D SPATIAL SECTION

Linearized metric, plus polarization

\[ds^2=-c^2dt^2+[1+h_+(t)]dx^2+[1-h_+(t)]dy^2\]

\[h_{\mathrm{det}}=\frac{1}{2}h_{ij}(u^iu^j-v^iv^j)=F_+h_+ + F_\times h_\times\]

\[\frac{\Delta L_x}{L}=\frac{h_+}{2},\qquad \frac{\Delta L_y}{L}=-\frac{h_+}{2}\]

The drawing shows only the x-y plane crossed by the wave. It does not reduce general relativity to two dimensions.

3 | OPTICAL READOUT

Path-length and phase difference

\[h=\frac{\Delta L_x-\Delta L_y}{L}\]

\[\Delta\Phi\simeq\frac{4\pi L}{\lambda}h\]

The page uses L=4000 m and lambda=1064 nm in a simple low-frequency, normal-incidence approximation. Real detectors reconstruct strain from photodiode and control signals.

4 | SPECTROGRAM

STFT and sampled frequencies

\[S(m,k)=\left|\sum_n h[n]w[n-mR]e^{-i2\pi kn/N_{\mathrm{FFT}}}\right|^2\]

\[f_k=\frac{k f_s}{N_{\mathrm{FFT}}},\qquad f_{\mathrm{Nyquist}}=\frac{f_s}{2},\qquad \Delta f=\frac{f_s}{N_{\mathrm{FFT}}}\]

Color indicates relative power. File timestamps indicate when records were generated and do not form a uniform physical sampling clock.

5 | INSPIRAL, MERGER, RINGDOWN

Teaching CBC carrier

\[f(t)\propto (t_c-t)^{-3/8}\]

\[h_{\mathrm{ring}}(t)=A_0e^{-(t-t_m)/\tau}\sin(2\pi f_{\mathrm{ring}}t+\phi)\]

The inspiral law is regularized and joined to merger and ringdown. It is not a numerical-relativity template and cannot estimate mass or distance.

6 | PSD, ASD AND WHITENING

Units and normalization

\[\mathrm{ASD}(f)=\sqrt{\mathrm{PSD}(f)},\qquad \tilde h_w(f)=\frac{\tilde h(f)}{\sqrt{S_n(f)}}\]

For calibrated data, PSD has units of strain²/Hz and ASD has units of strain/sqrt(Hz). Curves remain relative here because real instrument noise is not included.
